# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in pyhttp!

- **Bug reports and feature requests** are very welcome — open an
  [issue](https://github.com/pyhttp2/pyhttp/issues). Please include your OS,
  how you installed pyhttp (installer, portable, .dmg, .deb, pip, Homebrew)
  and the version (Settings → About).
- **Security issues**: please mail dev@pyhttp.com instead of opening a public issue.
- **Pull requests** are not accepted at the moment: pyhttp is published under
  the [pyhttp Free License 1.1](LICENSE.md) (free for everyone, source
  published, modified versions may not be distributed), and the project cannot
  merge outside code without a contributor agreement. If you'd like to see a
  change, an issue describing it is the best route.

Questions: dev@pyhttp.com
